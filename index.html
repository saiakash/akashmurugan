VisionGuide

AR-guided repair · iOS + Android

Point a phone at physical equipment; it recognises the machine, tracks its 3D pose live, and pins step-by-step service instructions onto the real object.

  • Built the Unity application on ARCore and ARKit, detecting equipment and pinning live AR guidance onto it.
  • Integrated and debugged the on-device inference layer — native arm64 TFLite YOLOv8 detection and M3T 3D tracking behind a P/Invoke bridge — resolving GPU/NNAPI delegate, threading and lifecycle failures on real hardware.
  • Rebuilt viewpoint detection from a brittle hard-gate IoU matcher into continuous sum-IoU template retrieval with cascading RANSAC PnP thresholds, across 8 documented design iterations.
  • Built the Workflow Editor and 3D CAD viewer in React: node-graph procedure authoring, Three.js CAD labelling, multi-tenant organisations with role-based access.
  • Shipped 5-language localisation with custom Indic ligature shaping, plus 80 automated tests and a frame-replay harness for off-device regression testing.

X-ray PCB Inspection

Industrial defect detection

Detecting components and faults in X-ray imagery of circuit boards — the most rigorous model work I have done, and the easiest to prove.

  • Trained and tuned detection models to identify components and defects, comparing architectures and training strategies to select the production model.
  • Solved long-tail class imbalance by generating Blender CAD renders and 3D Gaussian Splatting synthetic imagery plus background negatives, blended with real captures.
  • Built the dataset and training platform in Flask and JavaScript: ingestion, annotation-format conversion, class balancing, augmentation, splitting, training, evaluation analytics and multi-format model export.
  • Built pose-driven video stabilisation that warps frames by homography from ARKit rotation and intrinsics — it never looks at pixels, so moving tracked objects cannot corrupt it.

A full retail operations product running on cameras the store already had — no new hardware. Model, backend and dashboard.
